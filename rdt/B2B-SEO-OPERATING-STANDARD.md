# ResideTerra B2B SEO/AEO Operating Standard

## Permanent mandate

ResideTerra's organic program must grow both consumer demand capture and qualified B2B / professional acquisition on every future planning cycle. This is a permanent operating standard.

## Audience classification

Every Stage 02 content job must explicitly classify the primary audience as one of:

- `consumer`
- `hybrid`
- `professional_commercial`

Every manifest must also explicitly decide whether B2B intent applies.

If B2B intent applies, the manifest must define:
- professional personas
- professional/project context
- procurement support context
- the live Partner Concierge conversion target
- professional-section injection enabled for new jobs

If B2B intent does not apply, the manifest must provide a substantive reason. Never silently default B2B applicability to false.

## Content mix

Maintain at least 20% of the rolling roadmap as Professional / Commercial intent. Hybrid articles can increase the effective B2B footprint beyond that floor.

Do not create a separate generic B2B blog. Professional content belongs inside the canonical topical hub so authority compounds in the same category architecture.

## Article standard

When B2B applies, the article must include a useful Professional and Commercial Project Considerations section. It should add category-specific value around specification, installation, project planning, procurement, model selection, lifecycle, or trade coordination. It must not be generic sales copy.

Consumer usefulness, accuracy, and search intent remain primary.

## Conversion path

Qualified professional traffic routes to:

https://resideterra.com/pages/pro-partner-program

This is the Partner Concierge path for project-scale product selection, specification support, coordinated purchasing, and professional assistance.

## Research standard

Where relevant, research must include professional modifiers and intents such as:

commercial, trade, contractor, builder, architect, designer, landscape architect, pool builder, hospitality, resort, multifamily, developer, property manager, specification, cutout, rough-in, procurement, bulk, project, studio, facility.

## Category coverage

Every major revenue category and brand should eventually develop a professional branch. Typical node families include:

- specification and sizing guides
- cutout / rough-in / installation planning
- builder / designer / contractor guides
- hospitality and multifamily project planning
- commercial product comparisons
- procurement and bulk-purchase guidance
- project checklists
- lifecycle / service / ownership planning

## Measurement

Track:
- organic entrances to Professional / Commercial and Hybrid pages
- Partner Concierge assists and leads
- quote / project inquiries
- assisted revenue
- commercial-query rankings and impressions
- trade-relevant backlinks and citations
- category-level professional organic growth

## Governance

The Knowledge Graph and rolling rotation are the source of planning truth. Stage 02 is the enforcement layer. The B2B strategy must survive individual campaigns, personnel changes, and roadmap refreshes.
