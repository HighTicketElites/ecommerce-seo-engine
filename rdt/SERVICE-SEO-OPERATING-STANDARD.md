# ResideTerra Service-Intent SEO/AEO Operating Standard

## Permanent mandate

ResideTerra must capture project demand before a shopper knows the exact brand or product. This is a permanent organic acquisition layer for homeowners and professionals.

## Core funnel

Project-start query → useful planning article → relevant service page → guided shopping / project planning → product selection / quote / purchase.

## Rolling content floor

Maintain roughly 10–15% of the rolling roadmap as direct Project-Start / Service-Intent content. B2B / professional articles may overlap with this layer when they genuinely support a service path.

## Approved primary service destinations

- Electric Fireplace Design: https://resideterra.com/pages/electric-fireplace-design
- Outdoor Kitchen Design Service: https://resideterra.com/pages/outdoor-kitchen-design-service
- Partner Concierge: https://resideterra.com/pages/pro-partner-program

## Stage 02 manifest rule

Every new article must explicitly declare whether service intent applies.

If service intent applies, the manifest must define:
- service stage: project_start, solution_research, service_evaluation, or professional_procurement
- whether it is a direct service article
- one approved primary service destination
- substantive CTA context explaining why the service is the natural next step

If service intent does not apply, the manifest must include a substantive reason. The system must never silently default service applicability to false.

## Content architecture

Do not create a generic services blog by default. Service-intent content stays inside the relevant canonical category hub so topical authority compounds with product and technical content.

The service landing page should own the head service term. Supporting articles should target planning, budget, layout, specification, installation, and project questions around that service.

## Content quality

Do not publish thin advertorials such as "Why choose our design service." Direct service-intent articles must solve a real problem first: design, budgeting, layout, utilities, fit, installation, specification, or procurement.

## Linking standard

Direct service-intent articles must include the primary service destination. Supporting links can include category hubs, collections, active products, and closely related technical articles.

Professional articles can also route to Partner Concierge when relevant.

## Measurement

Track:
- service-intent organic entrances
- click-through to service pages
- form starts and completions
- guided-shopping engagement
- Partner Concierge assists
- quote requests
- assisted revenue
- rankings and impressions for project-start and service-intent queries

## Governance

The Knowledge Graph and rolling roadmap are planning truth. Stage 02 is the enforcement layer. Service-intent growth must survive roadmap refreshes, campaign changes, and personnel changes.
